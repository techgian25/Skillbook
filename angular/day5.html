Day 5: Angular Directives

What are Angular Directives?

Directives are special instructions in Angular that modify the behavior or appearance of DOM elements. They allow dynamic control over HTML structure and styling.

Angular has three main types of directives:

  • Component Directives
  • Structural Directives
  • Attribute Directives
Structural Directives

Structural directives change the structure of the DOM by adding or removing elements.

Examples:

  • *ngIf
  • *ngFor
  • *ngSwitch

Attribute Directives

Attribute directives change the appearance or behavior of an element.

Examples:

  • ngClass
  • ngStyle
